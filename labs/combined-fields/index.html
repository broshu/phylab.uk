Combined Fields: how it works

Magnetic field only: circular motion at constant speed

The Lorentz force does no work and provides the centripetal force:

The period does not depend on the speed or the radius. A negatively charged particle circles in the opposite direction.

Electric and magnetic fields superposed

When the electric force and the Lorentz force balance, the particle moves in a straight line at constant speed:

If they do not balance, the particle follows a cycloid-like curve; when gravity acts, must also be included in the net force.

Sequential fields: treat each region separately

The electric and magnetic fields occupy different regions one after another. In each region the particle performs one kind of motion, and the speed and direction at the boundary link one region to the next.
