AR Electric Field — Particle gun

Print on plain white paper and lay this marker flat where you want the beam to start. Print at 100% (actual size): the pattern should measure 4 cm × 4 cm — check with a ruler, and keep the white border.

⚛Particle gun
barcode value 12
How to use
1. Print the + / − charge markers too (Flat or Cube scheme) and place them in view.
2. Open the AR app, tap Start camera, and aim at this marker — it continuously fires test particles up out of the marker.
3. The particles feel the field of any visible charges and curve along it, leaving glowing trails (their motion curves).
4. Tune in the URL if needed: ?pspeed= launch speed, ?pforce= field strength, ?prate= particles/sec, ?pcharge=-1 for negative test charges.
