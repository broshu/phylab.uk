AR Electric Field — Charge Markers

Print on plain white paper. Keep the white border around each square — do not crop it. Cut the two markers apart so you can move them independently.

+Positive charge
barcode value 0
−Negative charge
barcode value 1
How to use
1. Open the AR app on an iPad in Safari (over HTTPS) and tap Start camera.
2. Point the camera at the + marker to see field lines radiating outward; at the − marker to see them converging inward.
3. Place both markers in view at the same time (a few cm apart) to see the dipole field flowing from + to −.
4. Good, even lighting and a flat, non-glossy surface give the most stable tracking.
