Why is it symmetric?

The numbers in the formulas update with the current parameters

1. Speed symmetry: equal speed at the same point

From v² − v0² = −2g y

|v| = √(v0² − 2g y)

The speed depends only on the height y, not on whether the object is rising or falling; only the direction is opposite.

2. Time symmetry: equal time through the same stretch

Rising A→B: vB = vA − gΔtup
Falling B→A: vA = vB + gΔtdown

Δtup = Δtdown = (vA − vB) / g

Think in reverse: the fall is the rise played backwards, and the acceleration is g in both.

3. Symmetry in the v–t graph

The graph is a straight line of slope −g, point-symmetric about (v0/g, 0).

The orange and blue shaded blocks: equal widths mean equal times; equal areas (one positive, one negative) mean the displacement has size AB in both; equal heights at the ends mean equal speeds.
