3D Magnetic Field Lab: help

Views

3D view: the default oblique view, like a textbook 3D diagram.

Side · along ab: look along the rod. The incline becomes a line and the rod shrinks to a point, which is the step used to draw a planar force diagram.

Top: see the circuit as a plan view.

The small window at the bottom right always shows the orthographic projection of the current view, so you can compare “3D” with “plane” directly.

Building a circuit

1. Click a part in the library on the left to add it, then place it with “Move” and “Rotate”.

2. Click “Wire”, then click the gold terminals on two parts in turn to join them with a wire.

3. Select a wire and press Delete to remove it; select a part to change its parameters.

4. When the rod rests on the rails, the rails are the wires, so no wiring is needed.

Quantities

Once the circuit is closed, the current direction and size are solved automatically and the Ampère force is found from F = BIL.

Orange F is the Ampère force, grey G is the weight and blue N is the normal force. Arrow lengths are drawn to scale.

Field angle α: 0° is straight down, 180° is straight up and 90° is horizontal to the right. When α = θ the field is perpendicular to the incline, pointing into it.
