How to read it

Deflection: the carriers drift along the current, and the Lorentz force pushes them to the same side of the slab. Charge builds up on the two edges, and the voltage between M and N grows.
Steady state: the electric force balances the Lorentz force, the carriers stop deflecting, and the voltage settles.
For the same current direction, positive and negative carriers are pushed to the same side, but the potentials are opposite. This is how the sign of the Hall voltage reveals the sign of the carriers. Reversing the magnetic field reverses the voltage.

⑤ Hall coefficient and applications

Define

Hall sensors measure magnetic fields and rotation speed. They are found in bicycle speedometers, computer cooling fans and tape-drive motors. Each time the wheel turns once, a magnet passes the sensor and produces one voltage pulse.

⑥ Which end is at higher potential

First use the left-hand rule (the force on a negative charge is opposite to the direction the fingers point) to find which side the carriers move to, then check whether that side collects positive or negative charge.
