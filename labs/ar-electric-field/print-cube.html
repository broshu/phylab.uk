AR Electric Field — Cube markers (+)

The charge sits at the cube's centre: the app steps every visible face inward by ½ the cube edge and averages them, so it stays dead-centre whatever face you see.

Calibrate the centre. The inward step is ?cube= (cube half-edge ÷ marker width). For a 7 cm box with 2.4 cm markers, that's 3.5 ÷ 2.4 ≈ 1.45 → open cube.html?cube=1.45. Default is 1.0.
