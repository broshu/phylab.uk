CUBE SCHEME · POSITIVE

AR Electric Field — Cube markers (+)

Print at 100% (actual size) — each black pattern is 3.2 cm. Stick these five markers on five faces of a small box (top + four sides; leave the bottom). Keep the white border around each square. The charge sits at the cube's centre: the app steps every visible face inward by ½ the cube edge and averages them, so the charge stays dead-centre whatever face you see.

Calibrate the centre. The inward step is ?cube= (cube half-edge ÷ marker width). For a 7 cm box with 3.2 cm markers, that's 3.5 ÷ 3.2 ≈ 1.1 → open cube.html?cube=1.1. Default is 1.0.

How to use. Stand both cubes on a desk, lay the gun cards flat beside them, open Cube AR and aim at a cube. After it auto-locks, walk around to see the 3-D field; the shape stays rigid. Guns fire e⁺ / e⁻ along their printed arrow (rotate with ?gundir= if needed). The cube codes (0–9) are independent of the Plane scheme — only run one scheme at a time.
