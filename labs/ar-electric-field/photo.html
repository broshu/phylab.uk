Photo (static) Field lines

Lay the printed + / − charge cards (and the electron-gun cards) flat on a desk, take a photo from roughly above, then upload it here. The cards are detected once in the still image — completely stable — and the field lines, potential surface and electron-gun trajectories are drawn on top.

Uses the same plane markers as Plane AR. Best with a sharp, evenly-lit, near top-down photo.
