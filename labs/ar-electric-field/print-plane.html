AR Electric Field — Plane markers

Print at 100% (actual size) — each black pattern should measure 3.4 cm (check with a ruler). Cut the cards apart and lay them flat on a desk; keep the white border (quiet zone) around each square. The charge sits at the centre of its marker. These codes are independent of the Cube scheme.

How to use. Open Plane AR, tap Start camera (HTTPS, best on an iPad), and aim at the markers. + → field lines radiate outward; − → lines converge inward; both together → a dipole. The layout auto-locks after a moment, freezing the field shape so you can move around without jitter (tap Re-scan for a new layout). Each gun fires test charges along its printed arrow — the + gun fires e⁺, the − gun fires e⁻. If a beam comes out the wrong way, add ?gundir=1 (0–3 quarter-turns) to the URL.
