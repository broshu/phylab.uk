PLANE SCHEME

AR Electric Field — Plane markers

Print on plain white paper at 100% (actual size) — each black pattern should measure 4 cm × 4 cm (check with a ruler). Cut the cards apart and lay them flat on a desk. Keep the white border (quiet zone) around each square — that is what the camera locks onto. The charge sits at the centre of its marker. Codes here are independent of the Cube scheme.

How to use
1. Open Plane AR and tap Start camera (HTTPS, best on an iPad).
2. Aim at the + marker → field lines radiate outward; − → lines converge inward; both together → a dipole.
3. The layout auto-locks after a moment, freezing the field-line shape so you can move around without jitter. Tap Re-scan for a new layout.
4. Each electron-gun card fires test charges along its printed arrow: the + gun fires e⁺, the − gun fires e⁻. They bend along the field. If a beam comes out the wrong way, add ?gundir=1 (0–3 quarter-turns) to the URL.
5. Tracking is steadiest with even lighting on a flat, non-glossy surface.
