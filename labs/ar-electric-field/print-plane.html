AR Electric Field — Plane markers

Print at 100% (actual size) — each black pattern should measure 3.4 cm (check with a ruler). Cut the cards apart and lay them flat on a desk; keep the white border (quiet zone) around each square. On scan, each point charge floats above its sticker for a 3-D field. These codes are independent of the Cube scheme.

How to use. Open Plane AR, tap Start camera (HTTPS, best on an iPad), and aim at the stickers. On scan each point charge floats above its sticker and a true 3-D field appears. The layout auto-locks after a moment, freezing the field so you can walk around it without jitter (tap Re-scan for a new layout).
Charged plates. A plate sticker (4 / 5) raises a square board standing on the desk — 4× the marker edge wide and tall, perpendicular to the desk — carrying a uniform charge = 2× a point charge, running along the marker's printed arrow. Its field leaves both faces. Tune with ?lift= (charge height), ?plateside=, ?plateq=.
Electron guns. The + gun fires e⁺, the − gun fires e⁻ along the printed arrow; they bend through the 3-D field. If a beam points the wrong way, add ?gundir=1 (0–3 quarter-turns) to the URL.
