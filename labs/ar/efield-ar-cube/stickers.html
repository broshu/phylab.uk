Cube Charge Markers — Print & Download

All five faces use the same marker. Whichever face the camera sees, the field lands at the same cube centre. Print this sheet, cut out five tiles, and stick one on each face (leave the bottom for the post).

1 · Size the markers to your cube

Pick a width that fits your cube face with white space left over on all sides — the white border is the quiet zone the camera locks onto, so don't crop it tight. A good rule: black square ≈ 65–75% of the face edge. (e.g. a 70 mm face → ~50 mm marker.)

4 · Build the cube

  1. Stick one marker on each of the five faces (top + four sides). Leave the bottom free for the post.
  2. Centre each marker on its face and press it flat — no curling edges.
  3. Keep every marker the same way up isn't required; orientation in the plane doesn't matter for a point charge.
  4. Mount the cube on the post and stand it on the table.
  5. Open the app, tap Start camera, and aim at any face. Use the on-screen offset control (and "show cube guide") to dial the charge to the exact centre: set the offset so the wireframe box matches your real cube.

Offset rule of thumb: offset ≈ (half the cube edge) ÷ (marker black-square width). For a 70 mm cube with a 50 mm marker that's about 35 ÷ 50 = 0.70.
