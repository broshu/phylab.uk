FLAT SCHEME

AR Electric Field — Flat charge markers

Print on plain white paper, cut the two markers apart, and lay them flat on a desk. Keep the white border (quiet zone) around each black square — don't trim too tight, as the camera locks onto the marker using that border.

Flat — laid flat on a desk

With the markers flat, the field lines "grow" up out of the desk. Best viewed from above or at an angle.

Cube — stuck on a box

With markers on 5 faces you can walk all the way around and see the 3D field from any angle.

barcode value 0
−Negative

How to use
1. Open the AR app in Safari on the iPad (HTTPS required) and tap Start camera.
2. Aim at the + marker → field lines radiate outward; aim at the − marker → field lines converge inward.
3. Put both markers in view together (a few cm apart) → a dipole field pointing from + to −.
4. Tracking is most stable with even lighting and a flat, non-reflective surface.
