AR Electric Field — Cube charge (stuck on a box)

Print and cut out the markers below, then stick 5 different markers on the 5 faces of a small box (edge ≈ 2× the marker's black square, ~5 cm): the top + 4 sides. The bottom rests on the table and is left blank. Assemble & use
1. Find a small cubic box with an edge about 2× the marker's black square (≈ 5 cm); stick the 5 markers on the top and four sides per the labels above, keeping the white border around each. A different box size is fine — just add ?cube=value to the URL to adjust (default 1.0 = edge 2× marker).
2. Open the AR app in Safari on the iPad (HTTPS required) and tap Start camera — the scan button is shared with the flat scheme, so there's nothing to switch.
3. Put the + cube in view for an outward field; the − cube for an inward field; both together → a 3D dipole field.
4. The 5 distinct faces let the app fuse them into one cube and place the charge at the cube's centre, so you can hold the iPad and walk all the way around with a stable, jump-free field.
5. Use even lighting and avoid glare; tracking is most stable with the cube about 20–50 cm from the camera.
