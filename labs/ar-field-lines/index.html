AR Electric Field Lines

Point your iPad camera at the printed + or − charge marker to see its 3D electric field. Show both markers together to see a dipole field.

Requires camera access. Best in Safari over HTTPS.

First, print the charge patterns
▢ 平面方案 · Flat Print + / − tiles, lay them flat on a desk ⬢ 立体方案 · Cube Stick markers on 5 faces of a small box

Both schemes use the same scan button — the app recognises whichever pattern your camera sees, so there is nothing extra to choose here.
