How to read it

Deflection: when the particles first enter, the Lorentz force pushes positive ions up and negative ions down, and charge starts to build up on the plates.
Steady state: the electric force between the plates balances the Lorentz force, the particles stop deflecting, and the plate voltage settles.
The magnetic field points into the page and the velocity points right, so a positive charge feels an upward force.

MHD Generator: how it works

① Deflection: charge separation

A hot plasma (containing many positive and negative ions) enters the magnetic field with speed v. The field points into the page, so the Lorentz force pushes positive ions to the top plate and negative ions to the bottom plate.

The positive and negative charge collected on the plates sets up an electric field E0 between them.
