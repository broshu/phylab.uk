Electric Potential in 3D

Two charges create a potential field. On the left, the 2D plane shows field lines and equipotential contours; on the right, the same potential is lifted into a true 3D surface. The height is exactly V = Σ kq/r — nothing is clamped, so each charge becomes an infinitely sharp spike: a peak shooting up over a positive charge, a well plunging down at a negative one. Because a real 1/r singularity is infinitely thin, the spikes taper to invisible needles all on their own. Drag the charges on the left, or drag the 3D view to orbit.
