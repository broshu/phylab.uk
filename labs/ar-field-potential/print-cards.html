Charge cards

Print this sheet on plain matte paper (A4 or Letter), then cut out the cards. Each code is printed perfectly square — that matters, because the camera can only read a square 3×3 grid. Keep the white margin around every code (the quiet zone the camera needs), lay the cards flat, avoid glare, and use even lighting. Red = positive (+), blue = negative (−), green = electron gun. Each card has its own code, so you can scatter as many as you like.
