Charge cards

Ready-made A4 PDF ↗

Two pages, one copy each. Page 1 holds the positive (+) charge cards and the positive particle gun; page 2 holds the negative (−) cards and the negative particle gun. Print on matte paper, cut out the cards, keep the white border around every code, lay them flat with even lighting. Each gun fires test charges toward its ▲ arrow — the + gun fires + charges, the − gun fires − charges.
