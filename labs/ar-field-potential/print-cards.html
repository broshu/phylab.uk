Charge cards

Print this sheet (A4 or Letter) on plain paper, then cut out the cards. Each card carries its own 3×3 code, so you can lay out as many as you like — every visible card is tracked at once. Red cards are positive (+), blue cards are negative (−). Keep the white border around each code intact (it is the quiet zone the camera needs), lay the cards flat on a desk, and avoid glare. Then open the app and point your iPad at them.
