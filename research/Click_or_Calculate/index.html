Answer sheets

To protect the students' privacy, every page was processed in the same way:

  1. The class and name fields at the top right of the page were covered with a solid white box.
  2. The page was reduced to 420 pixels in width and blurred with a Gaussian filter (radius 2.6 pixels). At this level the amount and layout of the written work, the sketches and the trajectories can be seen, but the handwriting cannot be read.

Each page is labelled at the top right with the student's anonymous ID (for example, A05). The IDs are the same as those used in the score data; they contain no personal information.

The answer sheets of 29 students (Group A 13, Group B 12, Group C 4) are not included, because the students or their parents did not consent to publication. These answer sheets were scored with the same rubric and are included in all analyses; their scores and drawing codes are in the score file.
