## Why recommended

指出许多“ChatGPT 提高学习”研究把 AI 与其他教学因素混在一起比较，正是你三组 AI 作业实验和辩论实验在设计上必须避开的问题。

## Summary

作者逐一检查两篇 ChatGPT 元分析所纳入的媒介比较研究，看 ChatGPT 组与对照组在教学方法、练习量和学习时间上是否可比。结果发现多数研究缺乏控制或信息不足，因此 ChatGPT 的益处目前应谨慎解读。

## Key finding A

Many studies reporting that ChatGPT improves learning compared it with control conditions that differed in teaching method, practice or time. The benefits of ChatGPT should therefore be read with caution.

> 许多声称 ChatGPT 提升学习的研究，其对照组在教学方法、练习或时间上与实验组不同，因此 ChatGPT 的益处应谨慎看待。

## Key finding B

Re-examining the media-comparison studies in two 2025 meta-analyses, the authors checked whether ChatGPT and control conditions matched on instructional methods, practice with the outcome measure and time on task. Most comparisons were confounded or under-reported, including those behind the most common positive result.

> 作者复查两篇 2025 年元分析中的媒介比较研究，检查 ChatGPT 组与对照组在教学方法、对测验内容的练习和学习时间上是否匹配；多数比较存在混杂或报告不足，包括最常见的正面结果。

## PhD relevance

Use its three matching criteria (method, practice, time) as a checklist when designing the paper, general-AI and custom-AI homework groups.

> 在设计纸质、通用 AI、定制 AI 三组作业时，可把它的三条匹配标准（方法、练习、时间）当作检查清单。

## Reading recommendation

Read the coding criteria and the results table; cite it in your methods section to justify matched conditions.

## Limitations

A critical review of studies from two meta-analyses rather than new data; it shows that evidence is weak, not that ChatGPT is ineffective.

> 这是对两篇元分析所含研究的批判性复查，并非新数据；它说明证据薄弱，而不是证明 ChatGPT 无效。

## Abstract

Research on the use of artificial intelligence in education is rapidly accumulating. In the ChatGPT literature, two recent meta-analyses were published (Deng et al., 2025 and Wang & Fan, 2025) that examined media comparisons between ChatGPT and more traditional forms of instruction; both reviews found ChatGPT to improve learning. However, as researchers have found in media comparisons studies (e.g., Clark, 1983; Lawson et al., 2024), instructional methods are often not controlled between conditions, making it difficult to attribute any learning differences to affordances of the medium itself. Therefore, the purpose of our critical review was to examine those media comparisons studies included in Deng et al. (2025) and Wang and Fan (2025) to understand whether the ChatGPT and control conditions were comparable on their instructional features (i.e., matched on instructional methods, practice with the dependent measure, and time spent learning the content). Results indicated a lack of control in the ChatGPT studies that were reviewed, with many studies conflating the use of ChatGPT with other instructional features. Further, across the various direct learning outcomes, studies often did not include enough information to determine the comparability of conditions. For the most common learning outcome (i.e., ChatGPT > Control), comparisons often involved a confound between conditions as well as missing information about instructional features. Therefore, the benefits of ChatGPT must be interpreted with caution at the present time, and more research is needed to determine how, for whom, and under what pedagogical conditions ChatGPT can improve learning.

> 关于人工智能在教育中应用的研究正在迅速积累。在 ChatGPT 相关文献中，最近发表了两篇元分析（Deng 等，2025；Wang 和 Fan，2025），它们考察了 ChatGPT 与较传统教学形式之间的媒介比较研究；两篇综述都发现 ChatGPT 能促进学习。然而，正如研究者在媒介比较研究中发现的那样（例如 Clark，1983；Lawson 等，2024），不同条件之间的教学方法往往没有得到控制，因此很难把学习差异归因于媒介本身的功能。因此，我们这篇批判性综述的目的，是检查 Deng 等（2025）和 Wang 与 Fan（2025）所纳入的媒介比较研究，了解 ChatGPT 条件与对照条件在教学特征上是否可比（即在教学方法、对因变量测量内容的练习以及学习内容所花时间上是否匹配）。结果表明，所审查的 ChatGPT 研究缺乏控制，许多研究把使用 ChatGPT 与其他教学特征混在一起。此外，在各种直接学习结果上，研究往往没有提供足够信息来判断条件是否可比。对于最常见的学习结果（即 ChatGPT > 对照），比较往往存在条件间的混杂，并缺少关于教学特征的信息。因此，目前对 ChatGPT 的益处必须谨慎解读，还需要更多研究来确定 ChatGPT 如何、对谁、在什么教学条件下能够促进学习。
