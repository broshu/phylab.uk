## Why recommended

## Summary

把钕磁铁固定在沿斜面下滑的玩具车上，用手机 Phyphox 记录磁场随时间的变化，直接分析磁场的一阶和二阶时间导数。导数最大值随倾角系统增大，并与 sin θ 呈强线性相关。

## Key finding A

A smartphone magnetometer can serve as a motion sensor: a magnet on a toy car rolling down an incline produces a field signal whose time derivatives track uniformly accelerated motion.

> 手机磁力计可以当作运动传感器：沿斜面下滑的玩具车上装磁铁，其磁场信号的时间导数能反映匀加速运动。

## Key finding B

Recording a neodymium magnet on a toy car with Phyphox, the author analysed the field directly rather than converting it to displacement. Peak first and second time derivatives rose with incline angle and correlated strongly and linearly with sin theta.

> 作者用 Phyphox 记录玩具车上钕磁铁的磁场，不换算成位移而直接分析磁场。一阶、二阶时间导数的峰值随倾角增大，并与 sin θ 呈强线性相关。

## Abstract

Smartphone magnetometers are widely used for measuring static magnetic fields but have received comparatively little attention as sensors for investigating motion. I present a simple, low-cost experiment in which the magnetic field produced by a cylindrical neodymium magnet attached to a toy car is recorded by a smartphone running the Phyphox application as the car moves down an inclined plane. Rather than converting the magnetic-field data into displacement through calibration, the magnetic field is analysed directly as a function of time. The first and second temporal derivatives of the magnetic field are calculated numerically to investigate how the magnetic signal changes during uniformly accelerated motion. The maximum values of both derivatives increase systematically with the inclination angle and show a strong linear correlation with sin θ . The experiment combines mechanics, magnetism, numerical differentiation and data analysis within a single accessible activity. It demonstrates that a smartphone magnetometer can serve as a dynamic motion sensor while providing students with practical experience in experimental data acquisition and numerical analysis using inexpensive equipment.

> 智能手机磁力计被广泛用于测量静磁场，但作为研究运动的传感器却相对很少受到关注。我介绍一个简单、低成本的实验：把圆柱形钕磁铁固定在玩具车上，玩具车沿斜面运动时，用运行 Phyphox 应用的智能手机记录磁铁产生的磁场。实验不通过校准把磁场数据换算成位移，而是直接把磁场作为时间的函数进行分析。通过数值计算磁场的一阶和二阶时间导数，研究匀加速运动过程中磁信号如何变化。两个导数的最大值都随倾角系统性增大，并与 sin θ 呈很强的线性相关。该实验在一个易于开展的活动中结合了力学、磁学、数值微分和数据分析。它表明智能手机磁力计可以用作动态运动传感器，同时让学生用廉价设备获得实验数据采集和数值分析的实践经验。
