How this library is curated

The focus is on research that distinguishes conceptual understanding, experimental competence and independent transfer. Foundational frameworks and assessment tools sit alongside recent studies. PhD Core identifies essential reading for research design or theory; Read recommends the full text; Skim provides background context.

Recommendations are added after checking the original sources. This is a selective reading library, not a systematic review. Short-term scores, cognitive load and self-reports do not establish practical competence or long-term transfer. Brief findings introduce each paper; follow its card to read the original source and assess the evidence.
