Voltmeters & Ammeters02
Model

A galvanometer is sensitive — and limited.

Its movement reaches full-scale deflection at a small current I_g.

Three movement parameters
I_gfull-scale currentthe most it can carry
R_ginternal resistanceof the coil
U_gI_g R_g at full scalethe voltage it then reads
Voltmeters & Ammeters03
Compare

Series adds voltage range; parallel adds current range.

Same movement, two different jobs — set by where the extra resistor goes.

Voltmeter — series R_s

Big R_s drops most of the voltage → reads large U.

Ammeter — parallel shunt R_\mathrm{sh}

Small R_\mathrm{sh} carries the extra current → reads large I.

Voltmeters & Ammeters05
Derive

Pick R_s so full-scale current means full-scale voltage.

The series resistor takes whatever voltage the movement cannot.

U = I_g\,(R_g + R_s)
R_s = \dfrac{U}{I_g} - R_g

Larger desired U → larger R_s. That is why a voltmeter's resistance is high.

Voltmeters & Ammeters06
Summary
A voltmeter is a galvanometer in series with a large resistor; an ammeter is one in parallel with a small one. The resistor sets the range — and protects the movement.
